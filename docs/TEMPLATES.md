# Starter canvases

`src/templates.ts` defines starter canvases as data. A template has an ID, name, description, category, tags, preview metadata, canvas bounds, and one or more boards. Each board defines position, size, title, presentation, optional style overrides and budget, plus text elements and product placeholders. Child positions are relative to the board.

`instantiateTemplate` creates ordinary Cura `Outfit`, `Item`, and `BoardElement` records with fresh IDs and absolute item positions. The template definition is not needed after creation. The existing canvas persistence, board movement, resizing, restyling, deletion, and dissolution paths continue to apply. “New board” still makes one empty board; the Templates gallery creates a full starter composition and frames it in the viewport.

A placeholder is an `Item` with a `placeholder` value and a `templateCardSize`. It has no source URL, price, availability, or purchase state. Price summaries exclude placeholders. Clicking or tapping the card, its visible Add product button, or pressing Enter/Space opens the same replacement dialog. Desktop right click also offers Replace item. Link replacement keeps the item ID, position, board membership, and card size, then uses Cura’s product resolver. Manual replacement supports a name, brand, optional price, and uploaded image. Replacement removes the placeholder value and leaves a normal product item.

The initial curated library has 18 starters across Fashion, Beauty, Home, Travel, Planning, and Layouts. It includes category boards, parallel AM/PM and comparison boards, sequential routines, a vertical outfit composition, priority zones, image-first rooms, and pure layout canvases. The picker offers a curated For You view plus category filters. Each card shows a lightweight SVG miniature generated from the template's board and child coordinates, board colors, name, short description, category, and board/spot counts. Selecting a card opens a larger preview and board list; only Use template instantiates it. On phones, tapping a card opens a dedicated detail view with a back action. New board remains a separate empty-board action. No template creates fake products or prices. Hair Routine is an optional organizational framework; its steps are not required care instructions. Cura has no general undo history yet, so template creation and replacement use the same editing behavior as existing board actions.

## Sections

A template can declare Sections with a title, optional subtitle, position, size, padding, visual style, Board references, and optional direct placeholders. Instantiation stores each Section as its own canvas record with fresh references to ordinary Cura Boards and Items. It has no budget, product total, or Board membership. Existing canvases without Sections continue to load normally. The picker draws Section frames behind their Boards from the same configuration.

Sections appear in Capsule Wardrobe, Hair Routine, Vacation Packing, and New Apartment; other templates remain unframed. Select a Section to edit its title, subtitle, and style. At phone widths the controls appear in a compact editor outside the scaled canvas. Drag its header handle to move its referenced Boards and Items together, or resize its frame without resizing inner Boards; arrow keys also nudge the focused handles. Inner Boards still move and resize independently. **Remove frame** keeps the contents in place; **Delete group** removes that Section and its referenced Boards and Items. Both actions are inline. Local canvas persistence and editable shared copies store the instantiated Section and its references, so the original template definition is not needed afterward.

## Selecting and removing Boards

Click a Board title to select and edit one Board. Use Ctrl/Command/Shift-click or a Board's **Select** control to add or remove it from multi-selection. The **More** menu can open the Board selection bar or select all Boards; its scrollable Board list also works on touch screens. When two or more Boards are selected, the bar offers **Delete selected boards**. When every Board is selected, it reads **Delete all boards**.

This action removes only Board containers and Board membership. All Items keep their canvas coordinates and product data, including free-standing Items. Sections remain, even when all their referenced Boards have been removed; users can remove an empty frame separately. The existing **Delete board and all items** command remains a separate, clearly destructive per-Board action. Cura does not yet have a general Undo history, so Board-container removal has no Undo toast.
